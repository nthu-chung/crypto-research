CONCLUSIONS & FINAL ASSESSMENT
================================================================================

1. SIGNAL VALIDITY
   The Taker Buy Volume ratio (flow_z) shows a consistently NEGATIVE
   relationship with forward returns across BTC and ETH. This is the
   'contrarian' pattern: high buying pressure in the current bar predicts
   below-average returns in subsequent bars (mean reversion).

2. STATISTICAL ROBUSTNESS
   - Newey-West HAC tests account for serial correlation and heteroskedasticity.
   - If beta coefficients are significant pre-correction, they represent
     genuine (though small) predictive effects.
   - Multiple testing correction (Bonferroni/BH) is critical: with ~24 tests,
     ~1.2 false positives expected at alpha=0.05 by chance alone.

3. ECONOMIC SIGNIFICANCE vs STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE
   R2 values are very low (typical for 1h crypto returns).
   Statistical significance does NOT guarantee tradeable edge after
   transaction costs, slippage, and market impact.

4. DATA SNOOPING RISK
   - Sub-period analysis: If the signal is only significant in some periods,
     it may be period-specific (data snooping / regime-specific effect).
   - Sign consistency across all sub-periods is the strongest evidence.
   - Survivorship: Using BTC/ETH (most liquid, survived) may overstate signal.

5. MECHANISTIC INTERPRETATION
   Negative IC plausibly reflects: (a) micro-structure effects -- large buy
   flow causes temporary price impact that reverses; (b) contrarian liquidity
   provision dynamics; (c) retail FOMO behavior that reverses. These are
   theoretically coherent, supporting the signal's validity.

6. FINAL VERDICT
   Based on rigorous statistical testing:
   - SHORT-HORIZON (1h): Most likely to be statistically significant and
     mechanistically sound (micro-structure mean reversion).
   - LONGER HORIZONS (4-6h): Effect likely attenuates; less reliable.
   - POST-CORRECTION: Only the strongest findings (BTC/ETH 1h) are likely
     to survive multiple testing correction.
   - RECOMMENDATION: Use as a weak contrarian factor, combine with momentum
     and vol filters. Requires live forward testing before deployment.
